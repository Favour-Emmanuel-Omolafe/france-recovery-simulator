## 📌 Executive Summary
As of October 2026, France faces a severe macroeconomic trilemma: a bloated national debt (120% of GDP), a budget deficit of 5.4% (violating EU limits), and a highly volatile social climate where aggressive austerity measures risk triggering nationwide strikes. 

This project is a **Python-based interactive prescriptive analytics engine** built to solve this exact crisis. It allows policymakers and analysts to stress-test €60B in budget adjustments, mapping the cascading effects on GDP growth, debt trajectory, and social stability over a 10-year horizon.

## ⚙️ The Macroeconomic Engine
Unlike static financial spreadsheets, this algorithm accounts for dynamic human behavior and fiscal multipliers:
* **The Fiscal Multiplier Effect:** Slashing public sector jobs reduces the deficit but directly penalizes GDP growth (as consumption drops). Conversely, corporate tax cuts increase the short-term deficit but stimulate long-term GDP growth via business investment.
* **The Strike Risk Index (0-100):** A custom algorithmic threshold. If unpopular policies (like pension cuts or public sector layoffs) push the index above 75, the model triggers a "General Strike Drag," severely reducing GDP growth and widening the bond yield spread.
* **Market Panic (OAT Spread):** Bond yields react dynamically. If the deficit breaches the EU's 3% limit or social unrest spirals, borrowing costs mathematically increase, accelerating the debt snowball.

## 🤖 The AI Auto-Solver (Prescriptive Analytics)
This simulator elevates itself from descriptive to **prescriptive analytics** via a built-in optimization engine. Using Python's `itertools`, the application tests thousands of policy combinations in the background to find the optimal fiscal path. 

With one click, it mathematically hunts for the precise combination of tax cuts, budget cuts, and social investments required to bring the deficit under the EU's 3% mandate by 2036 *without* triggering a general strike.

## 📊 Interactive Policy Levers
- **Economic & Fiscal Adjustments:** Administrative Savings, Public Sector Job Cuts, Pension Reform, Corporate Tax Cuts.
- **Social & Demographic Buffers:** Hospital Reinvestment, Student/Youth Grants.
